A message meant for you

You are worthy.

And God loves you.

Not the polished version. Not the version you’re still working to become. You — right here, right now, exactly as you are.

Look up for a moment

“He determines the number of the stars and calls them each by name.”

Psalm 147:4

Every light above you was placed on purpose. The same God who named every one of them knows your name too — and He has never once forgotten it.

Hold on to this

Four things that are true about you today.

You are loved.

Not for what you do, or what you’ve fixed, or what you’ll prove. Loved completely, and for good.

“I have loved you with an everlasting love.”

Jeremiah 31:3

You are known.

Every detail. Every worry you haven’t said out loud. Nothing about you is hidden from Him, and nothing makes Him turn away.

“Indeed, the very hairs of your head are all numbered. Don’t be afraid; you are worth more than many sparrows.”

Luke 12:7

You were made on purpose.

You are not an accident, a mistake, or an afterthought. You were made with care and intention.

“I praise you because I am fearfully and wonderfully made.”

Psalm 139:14

You are never alone.

On the loudest days and the quietest nights, He is right there with you. Even now. Especially now.

“Never will I leave you; never will I forsake you.”

Hebrews 13:5

If no one has told you lately

Whatever you’re carrying today —

Maybe you feel like you’ve failed one too many times. Maybe you feel unseen, or like you’re too much, or not enough. Maybe it’s been a long time since anyone said this to you.

So hear it now:

Nothing you’ve done, and nothing that’s been done to you, can put you out of reach of His love.

“…neither height nor depth, nor anything else in all creation, will be able to separate us from the love of God that is in Christ Jesus our Lord.”

Romans 8:39
Breathe inBreathe out

Let it land.

Take one slow breath. Then say it to yourself, out loud if you can:

I am worthy. I am loved.

If tonight feels too heavy

You don’t have to carry it alone. In the U.S., call or text 988 any time to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Anywhere else, findahelpline.com lists free, confidential support near you.
